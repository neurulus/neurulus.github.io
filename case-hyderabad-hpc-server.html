University of Hyderabad

Provide a computing foundation for research.

A Neurulus HPC server for the University of Hyderabad’s School of Computer and Information Sciences.

School of Computer and Information Sciences

The challengeWhy NeurulusHow the platform solved itThe outcome

The challenge.

Research work needs computing resources that can be placed within the institution’s environment and used for its workloads. The starting point is a system the institution can own and operate.

Why Neurulus fit.

The institution needed dedicated computing capability within its own research environment. Neurulus fit by supplying a platform system around that requirement, with a dedicated HPC server the institution could place within its operating setting.

How the platform addressed it.

Neurulus deployed its platform for the University of Hyderabad’s School of Computer and Information Sciences with a dedicated HNS2600BPBX HPC server set. The compute system formed the foundation for the engagement’s institutional research workload.

The deployment put the computing resource in the institution’s own environment. Its role was to provide a concrete system around which the team could run and organise its research computing.

In the deployment

  • One Neurulus HNS2600BPBX HPC server set.
  • Institutional research-computing supply scope.
  • Delivery to the School of Computer and Information Sciences.

The outcome.

The institution gained a dedicated Neurulus HPC server within its research environment. The engagement established a computing foundation owned and operated in the institution’s setting.
